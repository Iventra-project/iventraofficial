## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered for modern campus committees, student organizations (BEM/HIMA), and creative youth communities across Indonesia. It pairs high-energy startup velocity with an airy, ultra-clean, and structured editorial layout. The emotional objective is dynamic professionalism: eliminating bureaucratic student paperwork while imparting an organized, premium, and seamless event execution experience.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Minimalism** with **Hyper-Clean Soft Elevation**. Interfaces prioritize luminous white canvases (`#FFFFFF`), rhythmic horizontal breathing room, deep navy typographic anchors, and electric cobalt gradients. Visual momentum is sustained through elevated floating micro-elements—such as contextual WhatsApp notification pills, live attendance badges, and soft tinted cards that mimic modern glass-like surfaces without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette is anchored around an electric indigo-cobalt spectrum complemented by deep typographic navy and soft structural tints.

- **Primary & Secondary Gradient Foundation**: The signature linear gradient is composed from `primary` (`#0C1DF3`) to `secondary` (`#414DE1`) at a 135-degree angle. This is reserved strictly for high-prominence touchpoints: main action buttons ("Buat Event", "Publikasikan"), active navigation indicators, key metrics, heroic promotional banners, and radiant background glow layers.
- **Accent & Borders**: Solid `#0C1DF3` provides sharp outline focus, hyperlinked cues, and active outline strokes.
- **Tint Canvas & Surface Containers**: Tertiary `#F0F2FF` is deployed as a structural tint for auxiliary surfaces, category tags, unread notification rows, and soft card container fills.
- **Neutrals & Typography**: Headings and pivotal data points utilize Dark Navy `#14142B` for uncompromising legibility and punch. Muted narrative text, table secondary rows, and metadata utilize Slate/Gray `#6B7280`. System background canvas remains pure `#FFFFFF` with muted panel dividers rendered in `#EAEBFA`.
- **System Signals**: Emerald `#10B981` is integrated selectively for Indonesian digital ecosystem familiarity (e.g., "WhatsApp Terkirim", "Tiket Terbit", "Lunas").

## Typography

Typography relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies to reflect an energetic, modern, and readable presence. 

- **Headlines**: Rendered in Bold (`700`) and ExtraBold (`800`) weights with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`). Headlines command instantaneous authority, driving visual scanning on event metrics, organization dashboards, and landing page hero units.
- **Body & Editorial Copy**: Set between regular weights (`400` and `500`) with proportional line heights to guarantee comfortable reading in Bahasa Indonesia, where event briefs and organizational rules tend to contain longer compound words.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Designed in Medium or Semibold (`600` to `700`) with neutral to slightly expanded tracking (`0.01em` to `0.04em`) to ensure rapid recognition on event status chips, badge counters, and metadata tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout architecture follows a structured 12-column fluid grid system on desktop screens, consolidating to 8 columns on tablets and 4 columns on mobile viewports.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12 columns, `margin` of `3rem` (48px), and `gutter` of `1.5rem` (24px). Maximizes breathing room with broad negative space bordering key modules.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, `margin-tablet` of `2rem` (32px), and `gutter` of `1.25rem` (20px). Event grids drop from 3-up to 2-up displays.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin-mobile` of `1.25rem` (20px), and `gutter-mobile` of `1rem` (16px). Cards span the full usable width; navigation transforms into a streamlined bottom dock or floating quick-action pill.
- **Spacing Scale Application**:
  - `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) govern compact component internals: pill padding, badge icons, and input helper spacing.
  - `space-md` (16px) establishes internal padding for list rows, status pills, and compact cards.
  - `space-lg` (24px) dictates comprehensive card container padding, section component gaps, and standard module splits.
  - `space-xl` (40px) controls cross-section vertical margins, ensuring generous vertical rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews muddy gray drop shadows in favor of ambient blue-tinted elevation and radiant backdrops.

- **Level 1 (Card Resting / Base Surface)**: Bordered with a thin, crisp 1px stroke of `#EAEBFA` and grounded by an ambient glow shadow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(12, 29, 243, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover Lift / Floating Cards)**: On hover, cards lift `4px` with an expanded tinted aura: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(12, 29, 243, 0.10)`. Borders transition subtly to solid `#0C1DF3` at 20% opacity.
- **Level 3 (Floating Status Chips & Overlays)**: Components like floating WhatsApp notification toasts, tooltips, and flyout selectors utilize: `0px 16px 40px -6px rgba(12, 29, 243, 0.14)`, accompanied by a subtle backdrop blur (`12px`) for high-contrast clarity against active content.
- **Heroic Gradient Glows**: Key promotional blocks, metrics cards, and CTA sections deploy diffuse pseudo-element radial glows (`radial-gradient(circle, rgba(12, 29, 243, 0.15) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0) 70%)`) positioned behind mockups and hero units to simulate forward projection.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an ultra-modern, inviting shape language defined by broad corner curves and balanced organic silhouettes.

- **Standard Elements (roundedness: 2)**: Form fields, nested data panels, event listing modules, and actionable dialogue containers adopt an outer radius between `20px` and `24px` (`rounded-xl` equivalent in this paradigm).
- **Interactive Badges & Buttons**: Interactive action triggers, tag chips, and notification pills employ fully pill-shaped contours (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to maintain contrast against rectangular media and calendar views.
- **Visual Mockups & Image Containers**: Event banners and dashboard mockups are finished with a `24px` radius, complemented by an internal 1px nested inset border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`) to retain crisp edge fidelity against light backgrounds.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Employs `linear-gradient(135deg, #0C1DF3 0%, #414DE1 100%)` with bold white text (`#FFFFFF`), full pill radius (`9999px`), and a radiant shadow: `0px 8px 24px -2px rgba(12, 29, 243, 0.35)`. Hover states trigger a subtle upward scale (`translateY(-2px)`) and increased shadow intensity.
- **Secondary Action**: Background `#F0F2FF`, solid `#0C1DF3` typography and icon tint, no border, pill-shaped. Hover states transition surface to `#E4E7FF`.
- **Outline / Ghost**: 1.5px border in `#0C1DF3` with transparent background and `#0C1DF3` text. Hover transitions to `#F0F2FF` fill.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category & Status Chips**: Pill-shaped with compact padding (`6px 14px`), background `#F0F2FF`, and text in `#0C1DF3` (`label-md`).
- **Live / Active Number Badges**: Gradient-filled pill containing white bold tabular figures, ideal for registration counters (e.g., "350+ Peserta").
- **WhatsApp Notification Pills**: High-visibility floating component with a subtle green accent beacon (`#10B981`), white surface base, and soft ambient shadow, displaying dynamic cues such as "Notifikasi WhatsApp Terkirim ke 120 Peserta".

### Cards & Event Panels
- **Event Card**: Pure `#FFFFFF` background, `20px` to `24px` border radius, 1px perimeter border `#EAEBFA`. Image containers sit flush with the top inner boundary. Hover activates a smooth `-4px` vertical lift accompanied by the Level 2 shadow.
- **Highlight Metric Card**: Surface tinted in `#F0F2FF` with deep navy numbers and micro-captions in `#6B7280`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs**: Height `48px` to `52px`, base background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E5E7EB`, border radius `14px` to `16px`. On focus: border shifts to solid `#0C1DF3` with a soft outer ring: `0 0 0 4px rgba(12, 29, 243, 0.1)`. Placeholder text formatted in `#9CA3AF`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes**: 20px x 20px with a 6px corner radius. Unchecked state features an outline of `#D1D5DB`. Checked state displays a solid primary gradient fill with a crisp white geometric checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons**: Dual-circle concentric indicator; active state displays a solid `#0C1DF3` outer rim housing a centered filled dot.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- **Feed Rows**: Edge-to-edge subtle division with `#F3F4F6`. Left-aligned avatar or event thumbnail with rounded corners (`12px`), followed by a two-line layout: primary title in `#14142B` (`label-lg`) and metadata time/campus tag in `#6B7280` (`body-sm`).

### Specialized Youth & Campus Modules
- **Committee Role Badges**: Color-coded pill micro-tags for fast role identification (e.g., "Ketua Panitia", "Sie Acara", "Humas").
- **Broadcast Status Banner**: Full-width or inline banner wrapped in the primary gradient (`#0C1DF3` to `#414DE1`) featuring high-contrast white text, pill CTA buttons, and subtle vector background curves for marketing and ticket drops.
